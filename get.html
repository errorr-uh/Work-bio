Opsdeck for Discord

No extension needed. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B shows it):

Opsdeck

Then open discord.com/app in the browser, click the Opsdeck bookmark, and press Ctrl+Shift+O. Click the bookmark again after every refresh.

Cannot drag? (phone or locked bookmarks bar)

Copy the code, create a new bookmark named Opsdeck, and paste the code as its address.

Works in the browser version of Discord only. Your account token is never read or stored. Using client mods is against Discord's rules, so you use it at your own risk.
